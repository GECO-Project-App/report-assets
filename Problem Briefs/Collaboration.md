# Collaboration

Introduction

Fostering Collaboration for Sustainable Community Development

•	Some rural communities on Gotland face economic challenges that threaten their sustainability. To address these issues, there's a need for increased collaboration among residents, businesses, and organizations. However, current collaboration levels are insufficient, hindering the community's ability to respond effectively to economic difficulties and achieve sustainable development.

•	In the rural communities of Gotland, the journey towards sustainability is being slowed down by a critical gap: the lack of effective collaboration. Despite the shared goal of sustainable development and the potential benefits it brings, individuals, businesses, and organizations are struggling to come together to make this vision a reality. This struggle stems from several challenges: a shortage of initiatives to kickstart collaboration, a reluctance or lack of interest among some community members to participate, and a noticeable deficit in knowledge about sustainability practices, particularly around forming energy communities.

o	The result is a community that, despite its potential, is unable to effectively address economic challenges or advance towards a sustainable future. This situation calls for a solution that simplifies the process of collaboration, making it more accessible and engaging for everyone involved, regardless of their current level of knowledge or interest in sustainability. An app designed to bridge these gaps could be the key to unlocking the collective power of Gotland’s rural communities, enabling them to work together more efficiently towards a sustainable and prosperous future.

Empathy-Driven Problem Statement

Even though everyone wants a greener, better future, they're finding it tough to work together to make it happen. It's like everyone's rowing in different directions - some folks aren't sure how to jump in and help, while others aren't even sure they want to. Plus, a lot of people just don't know enough about how to live more sustainably, especially when it comes to creating energy communities, which can really make a big difference.

Because of these hurdles, Gotland's communities aren't making as much progress as they could. They're stuck facing the same old problems, and the dream of a sustainable future seems far away. This calls for a simple, clever solution that brings everyone together, making it easy and fun for people to collaborate, no matter how much they know about sustainability right now. Imagine an app that clears up the confusion, sparks interest, and gets everyone on the same page. This could be the key to unlocking Gotland’s true potential, helping all of us work together to build a future that's not only greener but also richer and happier for everyone involved.

Target Audience / Stakeholders

•	The target audience for this project comprises older members of rural communities on Gotland, characterized by a blend of traditional values and open-mindedness towards new concepts, albeit with varying degrees of familiarity with technology. These individuals are predominantly retirees who value the democratic principles their communities stand on, emphasizing collective decision-making and active participation in community life. Their retirement provides them with the time to engage deeply in communal activities, fostering a strong sense of belonging and contribution.

•	This TA enjoys the social aspect of their communities, often gathering together for events, discussions, and projects that enhance the collective well-being of their surroundings. While they may not always be versed in the latest technological advancements, their openness to new ideas presents an opportunity to introduce and integrate the concept of energy communities in a way that resonates with their values and lifestyle. Their active involvement and influence within their communities make them essential stakeholders in the transition towards sustainable living practices, leveraging their collective experience, wisdom, and available time to contribute meaningfully to environmental stewardship and community resilience.
