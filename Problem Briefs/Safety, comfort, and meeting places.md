# Safety, comfort, and meeting places

Introduction

•	Enhancing Safety and Comfort in Rural Meeting Places

o	Some rural communities of Gotland, lack safe and comfortable meeting places for all community members. The absence of such spaces hampers community engagement and the development of long-term solutions to address local challenges. This issue is compounded by the need for safe and comfortable environments for all residents to feel included and valued in their communities.

•	Significance: 

o	When a small community does not have a place to meet there is high risk of that community growing distant or losing it’s sense of community. This is true for both not having a place to meet for events or other as well as not having a place to casually meet and see other members or the local community. Examples of this would be a building or room that belongs to the hembygdsförening or a local shop or café.

o	This problem affects everyone in a socken/community, even people who aren’t members of the local hembygdsförening or other organization.

Empathy-Driven Problem Statement

The need for environments where every resident feels included and valued is paramount. Whether it's for organized events or casual encounters, places like a community hall belonging to the hembygdsförening, or a local shop or café, serve as much more than physical locations. They are vital for fostering a sense of belonging, facilitating dialogue, and strengthening communal bonds. The impact of not having such spaces is far-reaching, affecting not just those actively involved in local organizations but every individual within the community. Addressing this gap is crucial for the health and vitality of rural communities in Gotland, ensuring they remain connected, resilient, and vibrant.

This absence significantly impedes community engagement and hinders the collective ability to develop long-term strategies for tackling local challenges. A community thrives on its ability to come together, share experiences, and collaborate on solutions. However, without spaces that invite and nurture such gatherings, there's a tangible risk of communities drifting apart or losing their sense of unity.

	
Target Audience / Stakeholders

•	Community Members:

o	The primary target audience consists of all residents of rural communities in Gotland, with a particular focus on older adults and families with children. These individuals value safety, accessibility, and a welcoming atmosphere in communal spaces. They seek opportunities for social interaction, community engagement, and participation in local events, which are best facilitated by comfortable and secure meeting places. Their experiences, both positive and negative, regarding existing meeting spaces shape their engagement with the community and their willingness to participate in its activities. They are invested in the well-being of their community and desire spaces where everyone feels welcome and safe to gather, discuss, and collaborate.

•	Local Organizations and Groups:

o	This includes entities such as the hembygdsförening, local clubs, activity groups, and other community-based organizations. These groups rely on accessible, safe, and comfortable meeting spaces to conduct their activities, host events, and foster community engagement. They need spaces that accommodate diverse activities and groups, support inclusivity, and are equipped to meet their operational needs. Their ability to effectively function and contribute to the community is directly tied to the availability and quality of local meeting places.

•	Local Government and Municipalities:

o	Local government and municipalities are key stakeholders responsible for the upkeep, development, and oversight of public spaces. They are concerned with ensuring that community meeting places are safe, accessible, and serve the needs of all residents. They need to consider planning, funding, and maintenance to ensure the availability of suitable spaces for community gatherings and events. Their decisions directly impact the quality and quantity of available meeting spaces and influence community cohesion and engagement.
