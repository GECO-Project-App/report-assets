1 GECO Card game

Description:

Prototype 1 is a game where up to two farmers face off against one player representing Gotland’s government. While the farmers try to raise their village’s attractiveness meter, the government tries to avoid overloading the energy grid and, at the same time, provide their city, Visby, and vital infrastructure with energy. Throughout the game, players pull cards that represent actions and events inspired by real life. A farmer's action could be to build a solar panel on their farm, which they have to pay money to activate. The government, on the other hand, could prevent the farmer from installing the solar panel by claiming it blinds overhead flying military planes, inspired by a real event that took place on Gotland.

Goal:

The aim of the game is to make people aware of the existing issues on the island of Gotland regarding the opposing goals of the government and the residents. While Sweden aims for Gotland to be completely autonomous in generating its energy, Gotland's energy grid is already exhausted in summer due to low energy usage, no ways to store generated energy, and the abundance of solar panels. Additionally, it is complicated for residents to apply for solar panels, and assistance often comes with a price. By using real events that happened on the island, we thought we could make people more aware of what options are available for receiving help and expose the flawed, overcomplicated system of the government in a satirical, less accusatory way.
