2 - Lighthouse Town/Fyrstadsspelet

Description and Goal:

Prototype 2 is a role-playing board game where players take on different roles of villagers trying to develop their village over the years. Inspired by Gotland’s history of evolving from a fishing island towards energy autonomy, we created a setting based on the real history of one of Gotland’s many fishing villages. One issue regarding the complicated application forms of Gotland’s government was that farmers did not receive enough help from their fellow villagers. We wanted to create a game that highlights the importance of communication in a village community. Additionally, we aimed to strengthen the bond between villagers by making them aware of their village’s long history and their impact on future generations living there. 

As players try to make their fictional village more modern and developed throughout the centuries, they take on different roles of the villages inhabitants each with different abilities. Using a village sheet as an overview, they must discuss which buildings to construct and finance, how much food is needed, and how many tourists and villagers to add to the village. Furthermore, at the beginning of each round, an event card is drawn, which provides the players with a topic to discuss. For example, a new fisher is joining the village, and they must decide whether to build an additional house for them or use their resources to build a solar panel instead.
